About

Mr Mohammad Eddama

Consultant Colorectal Surgeon

Mr Mohammad Eddama is a Consultant Colorectal Surgeon who sees patients with a range of benign colorectal and anal conditions, including haemorrhoids, anal fissures and anal skin tags.

He is committed to providing personalised, evidence-based surgical care, taking time to understand each patient's symptoms and priorities and explaining the options available so that decisions can be made together.

Alongside his clinical practice, Mr Eddama is actively involved in academic work at University College London, where he is a Clinical Principal Research Fellow in the Department of Surgical Biotechnology.
