About

Meet Mr Mohammad Eddama

Mr Mohammad Eddama is a Consultant Colorectal Surgeon offering private consultations for a range of benign colorectal and anal conditions, including haemorrhoids, anal fissures and anal skin tags.

Alongside his clinical work, he is a Clinical Principal Research Fellow in the Department of Surgical Biotechnology at University College London, where his research has included work on biomarkers, inflammation and surgical decision-making.
