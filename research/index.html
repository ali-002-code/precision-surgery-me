Research & Academic Work

Advancing Colorectal Care Through Research

Alongside his surgical practice, Mr Eddama is a Clinical Principal Research Fellow in the Department of Surgical Biotechnology at University College London. His published work spans biomarker research, inflammation and clinical studies in emergency and colorectal surgery.

University College London

Clinical Principal Research Fellow, Department of Surgical Biotechnology. Mr Eddama has held academic appointments at UCL since 2016, including Clinical Lecturer and Senior Clinical Research Fellow, and leads the “Doctor as Data Scientist” module at UCL Medical School.

Research interests

Themes across his published work

  • 01

    Biomarkers and liquid biopsy

    Studies of circulating extracellular vesicles and microRNAs as potential biomarkers, including for colorectal neoplasm and thyroid nodules.

  • 02

    Inflammation and epigenetics

    Laboratory research into histone demethylation and its possible role in Crohn's disease inflammation.

  • 03

    Emergency and colorectal surgery

    Clinical research on triage in acute surgery and on surgical decision-making in rectal cancer.
